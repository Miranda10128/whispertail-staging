Imagine a book written six hundred years ago — in a language no one on Earth can read.

In 1912, a book dealer named Wilfrid Voynich bought a strange manuscript from a Jesuit college in Italy. Carbon dating says its pages were made between 1404 and 1438. It has about 240 pages, covered in an alphabet that matches no known writing system on the planet.

Inside, there are drawings of plants that don't exist anywhere on Earth. Circular diagrams of stars and zodiac signs. And page after page of naked women bathing in strange green pools, connected by elaborate pipes.

The world's best codebreakers tried — including the men who cracked enemy codes in both World Wars. Nobody has read a single word.

Some say it's a lost language. Some say it's an elaborate hoax. It's been a hundred years, and the book still won't talk.

So what do you think — is the Voynich Manuscript a message from the past, or a joke that's lasted six centuries?
